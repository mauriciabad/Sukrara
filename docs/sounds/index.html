Alert sound test

Four packs, seven alerts, three sounds each. The app picks one of the three at random every time an alert goes off, so it doesn't get repetitive. Every sound is synthesised from code with original melodies.

The strip under each alert is its vibration on Android, drawn to scale from the haptic primitives (falls, thuds, clicks, rises; height = strength). It plays with the sound, and alone when the phone is on vibrate, so you can tell a low from a high in your pocket.

Lows ring on the alarm volume. In the app: Settings → Alerts → Sound pack, or one pack for a single alert. Source: spikes/alerts/sounds/gen.py.
