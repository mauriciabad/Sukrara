# Thresholds, severity and repeats (format: spikes/alerts/SPEC.md §7)

scenario Low is strictly below 70
 0  glucose 70
 1  glucose 69   => Low glucose
 2  glucose 70   => clear low
 4

scenario Very low is below 55 and wins over low; getting better doesn't alert
 0  glucose 55   => Low glucose
 1  glucose 54   => clear low; Very low glucose      # more severe: at once
 2  glucose 55   => clear very_low                   # back to low: no alert, the user knows
 3  glucose 54                                       # very low again within its repeat: quiet
 6               => Very low glucose                 # 5 min after the last alert
 7

scenario Highs at or above the threshold, each worse level at once
 0  glucose 179
 1  glucose 180  => High glucose
 2  glucose 250  => clear high; Very high glucose
 3  glucose 300  => clear very_high; Extremely high glucose
 4  glucose 299  => clear extremely_high
 5  glucose 179
 6

scenario High repeats every 15 min, very high every 10
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
15               => High glucose
30               => High glucose
31  glucose 260  => clear high; Very high glucose
41               => Very high glucose
45

scenario Repeat 0 alerts once per episode
settings high.repeat=0
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
30

scenario The alert goes when the reading gets old, then no data
 0  glucose 60   => Low glucose
 4  sensor off                     # last reading at 3
 5               => Low glucose
10               => Low glucose
15               => Low glucose
18               => clear low      # the last reading is more than 15 min old
23               => No glucose data
24

scenario A reading older than 15 min never alerts
 0  glucose 40 age 16
 1  sensor off
 4               => No glucose data   # but no data counts from it
 5

# LO / HI: the sensor's "below / above my range" (39 / 401 mg/dL, SPEC §1). Its real value is beyond every threshold,
# so it matches every low / high kind and the most severe one turned on alerts at once (no waiting for no data).

scenario LO is below every low threshold: very low at once
 0  glucose 80
 1  glucose 39   => Very low glucose          # LO
 2

scenario LO with very low turned off is low
settings very_low=off low=50
 0  glucose 39   => Low glucose
 1

scenario HI is above every high threshold: extremely high at once, whatever its threshold
settings extremely_high=450
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
 1  glucose 401  => clear high; Extremely high glucose   # HI: 401 < 450, still above it
 6               => Extremely high glucose               # its 5-min repeat
 7

scenario HI with extremely high turned off is the most severe high still on
settings extremely_high=off very_high=400
 0  glucose 401  => Very high glucose
 1

scenario A HI is never back in range by the margin: the high's mute holds while the sensor says HI
settings high=off very_high=off extremely_high=450
 0  glucose 401  => Extremely high glucose        # HI
 1  mute high    => clear extremely_high          # muted for 30 min
20                                                # 401 < 450 − 10, but a HI is no "back by the margin"
