# Episodes: one per side; it lasts until nothing of that side has matched for the hold time (the repeat
# interval, at least 5 min). Format: SPEC.md §7

scenario A brief exit keeps the episode: no new first alert, the repeat goes on
 0  glucose 60   => Low glucose
 1  glucose 75   => clear low
 4  glucose 60                   # back after 3 min out: same episode, the repeat is due at 5
 5               => Low glucose
 6

scenario A short exit keeps the treatments: the low after it still says eat more
 0  glucose 60   => Low glucose
 1  carbs 10     => clear low
 3  glucose 75
 5  glucose 60                   # out for 3 min: same episode, quiet until the carbs' window ends
16               => Low glucose · eat more
17

scenario Back in range for the hold time ends the episode: the next low starts plain
 0  glucose 60   => Low glucose
 1  carbs 10     => clear low
 3  glucose 75                   # the carbs worked
20  glucose 60   => Low glucose  # a new episode: at once, and the old carbs don't count
21

scenario Settings changed mid-episode
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  set low=60   => clear low    # 65 isn't low any more
 4  set low=70                   # low again within the hold time: same episode, due at 5
 5               => Low glucose
 6  set low.repeat=15
20               => Low glucose
21

scenario Switching alerts off clears everything; back on, it alerts again
 0  glucose 50       => Very low glucose
 2  set alerts=off   => clear very_low
 5                                 # nothing while off
 8  set alerts=on    => Very low glucose
 9

scenario An app restart (update) resumes the ongoing high without ringing again; the repeat keeps its time
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
 4  restart      => resume high  # shown again without sound (docs/15, "Two sounds at once")
15               => High glucose # the repeat, 15 min after the first
16
