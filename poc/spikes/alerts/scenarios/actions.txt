# The alert notification's actions (SPEC.md §2): Mute… (a picked or typed length) and Relax. They act on one side
# (lows incl. very low, highs incl. extremely high, …), never the other, and end at their time or once back in range by
# the margin (10 mg/dL for 10 min by default), whatever comes first. Mute all (the home screen, the Mac's menu) quiets
# everything. Format: SPEC.md §7.

scenario Mute quiets the low for its minutes; at once when it ends
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 5               => Low glucose
 7  mute low     => clear low                 # the notification's Mute 30 min
10                                            # would have been the repeat
37               => Low glucose               # still low when the mute ends: at once
42               => Low glucose
43

scenario Mute for… with typed minutes
settings mute.min=20
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  mute low 10  => clear low                 # the typed minutes, not the setting's 20
12               => Low glucose
17               => Low glucose
18

scenario Relax: the repeats come at the slower interval
settings relax.min=15                         # the default until round 22 (now 30)
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  relax low                                 # the notification stays
 5                                            # would have been the repeat
15               => Low glucose               # 15 min (the setting) after the last alert
30               => Low glucose
31
