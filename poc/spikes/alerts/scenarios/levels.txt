# Thresholds, severity and repeats (format: spikes/alerts/SPEC.md §7)

scenario Low is strictly below 70
 0  glucose 70
 1  glucose 69   => Low glucose
 2  glucose 70   => clear low
 4

scenario Very low is below 55 and wins over low; getting better doesn't alert
 0  glucose 55   => Low glucose
 1  glucose 54   => clear low; Very low glucose      # more severe: at once
 2  glucose 55   => clear very_low                   # back to low: no alert, the user knows
 3  glucose 54                                       # very low again within its repeat: quiet
 6               => Very low glucose                 # 5 min after the last alert
 7

scenario Highs at or above the threshold, each worse level at once

 5  glucose 179
 6
