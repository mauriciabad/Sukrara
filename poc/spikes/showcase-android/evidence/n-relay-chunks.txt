Conclusion: relay retention isn't the problem (no relay drops part of a paced 25-chunk burst; each keeps ≥ 28 h).
The thin history the emulator follower saw at 09:05 needs another cause; the plausible ones are timing on the follower
side: its single 12-s backfill ran before the wearer's chunks were on the relays (the approval's key delivery goes
out in the CONTROL lane ahead of queued chunks), or the open #c subscription was (re)sent after the first live event
with since = lastAcceptedAt − 15 min, which only matches the last ~2 hours. Fix (n-fixes): the wearer republishes 24 h
on every approval (and rotation, as before); the follower re-checks its last 12 h after a full backfill, looks again
after 90 s and then sends a `history` request (PROTOCOL-v2), which makes the wearer republish (≤ 1 / 10 min).
Side finding: the backfill also returns D's chunks from before the follower joined (older epoch, h-26…h-28 here);
the follower used to send a rekey request for them — now only events newer than its latest key trigger a rekey.
