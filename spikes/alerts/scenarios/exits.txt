# The ways out of a quiet alert (SPEC.md §2 "States and exits"; user, 2026-10-21: "There's no way to unmute! On any
# platform"): Unmute / Alert me anyway, Change (a new Mute on a muted episode), Stop slowing down. They act on this
# device only, like the notification's buttons. Format: SPEC.md §7.

scenario Unmute while still low: it alerts at once
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  mute low     => clear low                 # Mute 30 min
 6  unmute low   => Low glucose               # still low: at once, not at minute 32
11               => Low glucose               # the repeats count from there
12

scenario Unmute back in range: nothing now, at once if it's low again in the episode
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  mute low     => clear low
 3  glucose 90                                # in range: the episode holds 5 min after the last low reading
 4  unmute low                                # nothing to alert about
 5  glucose 65   => Low glucose               # same episode, not muted any more: at once
10               => Low glucose
11

scenario Unmute when nothing is muted changes nothing
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  unmute low
 5               => Low glucose               # the normal repeat, no extra alert
 6

scenario Change a running mute: shorter, then longer
 0  glucose 65    => Low glucose
 2  mute low 30   => clear low
 4  mute low 10                               # Change: until minute 14 instead of 32
14                => Low glucose
16  mute low 10   => clear low                # muted again: until 26
20  mute low 60                               # Change: until 80
80                => Low glucose
85                => Low glucose
86

scenario Stop slowing down: back to the kind's interval, an overdue repeat at once
settings slow.min=15
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  slow low
 5                                            # slowed: no repeat
 8  unslow low   => Low glucose               # the 5-min repeat was due at 5: at once
13               => Low glucose
14

scenario Alert me anyway lifts the carbs mute on this device; new carbs mute again
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 3  carbs 10     => clear low                 # quiet until 18
 5  unmute low   => Low glucose · eat more    # Alert me anyway: at once
10               => Low glucose · eat more
12  carbs 10     => clear low                 # a new treatment mutes again (until 27)
27               => Low glucose · eat more again
32               => Low glucose · eat more again
33

scenario Alert me anyway lifts the insulin mute on a high
 0  glucose 200  => High glucose
 2  insulin 2    => clear high                # quiet until 62
10  unmute high  => High glucose · still high after insulin
25               => High glucose · still high after insulin
26

scenario Unmute lifts a notification mute and a treatment mute together
 0  glucose 65   => Low glucose
 2  carbs 10     => clear low                 # quiet until 17
 3  mute low 60                               # and muted until 63
 6  unmute low   => Low glucose · eat more
11               => Low glucose · eat more
12

scenario The follower's Unmute shows the wearer's alert again at once
mirror
 0  glucose 65            => Low glucose; follower Low glucose
 2  follower mute low     => follower clear low
 5                        => Low glucose      # dropped on the follower
 7  follower unmute low   => follower Low glucose   # the wearer's alert is still up: shown here now
10                        => Low glucose; follower Low glucose
11

mirror

mirror
